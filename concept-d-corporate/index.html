A clinical-stage T-cell therapy company

Estrella is developing EB103, a CD19-directed ARTEMIS T-cell therapy, in STARLIGHT-1, a Phase I/II trial in relapsed or refractory B-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma. EB103 is investigational and not FDA-approved.

Two Receptors, One T Cell

  • The company says its AbTCR binds CD19 and signals through the T cell’s own CD3.
  • A separate co-stimulatory molecule adds a second signal.
  • Platform licensed from parent Eureka Therapeutics.
  • The company says, in comparisons with market-standard CAR-T constructs:
    • Significantly lower release of inflammatory cytokines such as IL-6
